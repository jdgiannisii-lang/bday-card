## Phase Goal

**As a** sender in the controlled cohort, **I want to** a maker that handles my photo and mistakes gracefully and hands me a clean shareable link with a QR, **so that** I can actually send the card, and **as the** founder **I want** a pre-committed ledger so the go/no-go is read mechanically, not rationalized after the fact.

<objective>
Harden the maker into something the cohort can really use, and author the VAL-04 decision instrument. Add client-side photo downscale + HEIC-failure handling (research Pitfall 3), inline validation and the UI-SPEC error states, the required consent checkbox and the optional who-sent-you field, and the Surface B share state with a QR code and copy-link feedback. Then write the pre-committed `docs/Stage-0-Decision-Ledger.md` carrying the D-03 KILL/ITERATE/CONTINUE thresholds, the generation-tree column schema, the verified-organic-propagation definition, and the ~30-day / minimum-10-engaged window, structured so the founder timestamps the thresholds before card #1.

This plan depends on Plan 01 (`maker.html`, `shared/supabase.js`) and Plan 02 (the PostHog event names recorded in `01-02-SUMMARY.md`).

Finally, add a Playwright UI smoke-test (`tests/ui-smoke.mjs`) that satisfies the repo UI-verification rule in `.claude/CLAUDE.md`: the full UI (maker form, card view, share state, CTA) only exists after this wave, so the smoke-test serves the static files, renders the maker, drives the create-to-open-to-CTA flow, asserts the engine canvas renders, and saves screenshots of each surface to a gitignored `tests/screenshots/` dir.

Purpose: Plan 01 proved the loop; Plan 02 made it measurable; this plan makes it usable by real testers, adds the required Playwright UI verification, and makes the go/no-go mechanical. Without the ledger there is no honest decision (the whole point of the GATE).
Output: `shared/photo.js`, the hardened `maker.html`, `docs/Stage-0-Decision-Ledger.md`, and `tests/ui-smoke.mjs` (plus a `.gitignore` entry for `tests/screenshots/`).
</objective>

Photo handling (research Don't Hand-Roll + Pitfall 3, all browser-native, no library):
- Downscale with `createImageBitmap(file)` then draw onto a `<canvas>` sized so the long edge is <= ~1600px, then `canvas.toBlob(cb, 'image/jpeg', 0.8)`.
- HEIC decodes natively only in Safari; on Chrome/Firefox `createImageBitmap` of an iPhone HEIC throws or yields a blank. Wrap in try/catch; on failure return a sentinel/throw the caller catches and shows the UI-SPEC upload error copy and asks for a JPEG. A WASM HEIC decoder is explicitly out of scope.
- The file input should `accept="image/jpeg,image/png,image/webp"` (research Pitfall 3a).

QR (research Standard Stack, major-pinned import): `import QRCode from 'https://esm.sh/qrcode@1'`, then `QRCode.toCanvas(canvasEl, url)` or `toDataURL` for the share-state QR of the `/c/<token>/` link. Fully client-side.

UI-SPEC copy (verbatim, no forbidden long-dash):
- Validation: `Add their first name` / `Write a note first`.
- Upload too large: `That photo's a little big. Try one under 10MB.`
- Upload failed/network: `That didn't go through. Check your connection and try again.`
- Save/link failed: `We couldn't make your link just now. Give it another try.` with a retry affordance.
- Share heading `Your card is ready.`; body `Send this link to their phone. They just tap it.`; `Copy link` -> transient `Copied`; QR caption `Or let them scan this.`; `Make another`; `Preview it`.
- Consent checkbox (required to enable submit): `I have permission to use this photo, and I understand this is an early experiment.` (Stage 0 spec section 5.2).
- Optional field: a who-sent-you-the-card-you-saw text input for soft propagation evidence.

D-03 thresholds for the ledger (verbatim intent): read on a ~30-day rolling window, minimum 10 engaged recipients. KILL = zero verified organic propagation across the cohort OR the finish-to-send rate upper 95% bound below ~15%. ITERATE (one round only) = tripwire alive but <=1 verified propagation. CONTINUE = >=2 independent verified organic-propagation events (2 distinct chains: 2 distinct non-recruited people who each received AND made their own). Verified means a gen-1 requester arrived via a tracked ref to a gen>=1 card AND names/pastes the card sent to them (self-report is soft evidence only). Cohorts: long-distance couples + college students (VAL-04). The thresholds + exact metric definitions are written into the ledger header and timestamped before card #1.

Only `maker.html` is shared with Plans 01/02 (this plan is wave 3 because of that overlap). Do not touch `card.html`/`404.html` here.

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-01 | Tampering | undecodable/HEIC photo upload | medium | mitigate | try/catch around createImageBitmap; typed decode error shows the UI-SPEC JPEG-ask copy instead of a blank or broken upload (research Pitfall 3) |
| T-03-02 | Denial of Service | oversized photo upload exhausting free Storage | low | mitigate | 10MB client cap + downscale to ~1600px JPEG before upload (research Don't Hand-Roll) |
| T-03-03 | Repudiation | decision rationalized after the fact | high | mitigate | Ledger thresholds are pre-committed and timestamped before card #1; the decision is read mechanically (D-03) |
| T-03-04 | Information Disclosure | consent for the uploaded photo | medium | mitigate | Required consent checkbox attesting permission to use the photo (Stage 0 spec 5.2); full moderation is the Phase 4 gate |
| T-03-SC | Tampering | esm.sh import of qrcode | low | mitigate | Major-pin `@1`; qrcode is OK in the research audit (mature, 15M+/wk); client-side only |
</threat_model>
