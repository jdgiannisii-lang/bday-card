Map of content for the **build-your-own animated greeting card** app idea,
sparked by the handcrafted birthday card in this repo (`index.html`).

## The one-line thesis

> **The tasteful, zero-friction card layer for people who are apart** — launched
> to long-distance couples, grown on a recipient-to-sender loop, monetized
> through gifting rather than the card.

## The wedge we're protecting

1. **Tasteful, non-templated craft** (not cheesy AI/template slop).
2. **Zero recipient friction** — opens in any browser, no signup, no install.
3. **A library of cards you've received** (retention + second viral surface).
4. **Beachhead:** long-distance couples + "just-because" sends (year-round).

## What's next (cheapest validation first)

- [ ] Landing page + waitlist for long-distance couples.
- [ ] Post the girlfriend card as a TikTok/Reel ("I built this instead of buying one"). Threshold: ~50k views or >20% waitlist conversion.
- [ ] Concierge test: hand-build 20–50 cards for strangers; measure how many ask to make their own (the viral coefficient).

## Open questions

- Does the craft actually convert recipients into senders?
- Will couples pay, and for what (premium effects vs. gifting vs. sub)?
- Is there a daily-ritual hook, or is card-sending inherently episodic?
- What share of the target couple base is both-iPhone?
